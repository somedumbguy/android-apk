## Full description (4,000 characters max)

> **Lift more. Log less.**
>
> Overload is a workout tracker built around progressive overload: the proven way to get stronger by adding a little weight or a rep every time you train. It works out your next weight for you, so you walk into the gym knowing exactly what to lift.
>
> **KNOW YOUR NEXT LIFT**
> • Every set is pre-filled with today's target weight and reps
> • Hit every rep and the weight goes up next time. Miss, and you repeat it
> • Stall three sessions in a row and Overload deloads 10% automatically, so you can build momentum back
> • Every suggestion comes with a plain-English reason
>
> **CHOOSE HOW HARD TO PUSH**
> • Cruise: small, steady jumps
> • Build: classic 5×5-style linear progression
> • Send it: bigger jumps for faster gains
> Switch anytime and watch your projected PR dates change instantly.
>
> **CHASE YOUR NEXT PR**
> • Your personal records, front and center
> • Forecasts for your next plate milestone: 135, 225, 315, 405 and beyond
> • A PR-attempt alert when today's weight beats your best
>
> **LOG A SET IN ONE TAP**
> • Tap a set to mark it done as planned. Tap the reps only when something changes
> • The rest timer starts automatically and buzzes when you're ready
> • Plates per side and warm-up sets, worked out for you
> • Linear progression for barbell lifts, and double progression (like 3×8–12) for dumbbells and machines
>
> **STAY CONSISTENT**
> • Workout streaks, weekly goals and a 7-day volume chart
>
> Whether you're starting your first 5×5 program or chasing a 4-plate deadlift, Overload handles the math so you can focus on the lift.
